Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tally collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Tally budgeting service and related SMS messaging features.

Information We Collect

  • Account data such as username, email address, and profile preferences.
  • Budgeting data such as categories, transactions, and savings goals.
  • SMS interaction data such as opt-in status, message responses, and delivery events.
  • Technical data such as device/browser details and basic usage analytics.

How We Use Information

  • To provide and improve budgeting tools and transaction categorization workflows.
  • To send service-related SMS alerts and receive your SMS responses.
  • To maintain security, detect abuse, and support account recovery.
  • To comply with legal obligations and messaging provider requirements.

SMS Privacy Commitment

"We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes."

Brand Name

The registered brand name referenced in this Privacy Policy is Tally.

Data Sharing

We may share limited information with vendors that help us operate Tally (for example, cloud hosting, analytics, and SMS delivery partners), only as needed to deliver the service and under contractual privacy protections.

Your Choices

  • You may request updates or deletion of account data, subject to legal obligations.
  • You may opt out of SMS messages by replying STOP when applicable.
  • You may request help for SMS programs by replying HELP when applicable.

Contact

For privacy requests or questions, contact the Tally support team at privacy@tally.example.
